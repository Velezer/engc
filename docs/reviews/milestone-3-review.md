# Milestone 3 Review

## Status

Accepted.

## Scope reviewed

- Technology-selection criteria.
- Artifact templates.
- Roadmap, README, changelog, validation, and traceability updates.

## Findings

- The milestone preserves technology-before-implementation by defining criteria and templates without selecting technologies.
- Technology-selection criteria require comparison against no-new-technology and minimal acceptable options before adoption.
- Artifact templates make classifications, traceability, Green Computing assessment, dependency assessment, and review notes repeatable for future specification work.

## Risks accepted

- Criteria may require refinement when detailed DSL and automation specifications expose more concrete evaluation needs.
- Templates are intentionally generic and may need specialized variants for compiler, linter, reviewer, CLI, and generator specifications.

## Next milestone

Milestone 4 should define detailed artifact catalog requirements for handbook, standards, checklists, playbooks, governance, metrics, and future specification families before DSL and automation specifications begin.
