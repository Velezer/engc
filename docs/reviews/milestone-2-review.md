# Milestone 2 Review

## Status

Accepted.

## Scope reviewed

- Architecture baseline.
- Architecture decision record requirements.
- Roadmap, README, changelog, and traceability updates.

## Findings

- The milestone preserves architecture-before-technology by defining boundaries and drivers without selecting implementation technologies.
- The architecture baseline links architectural drivers and quality scenarios to accepted requirement IDs.
- Decision-record requirements make Green Computing and dependency justification mandatory for future significant decisions.

## Risks accepted

- The architecture baseline is intentionally technology-neutral and will require later specification milestones before implementation can begin.
- Future DSL, compiler, linter, reviewer, CLI, and generator details remain unspecified.

## Next milestone

Milestone 3 should define technology-selection criteria and artifact templates needed before detailed DSL and automation specifications.
