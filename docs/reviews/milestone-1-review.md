# Milestone 1 Review

## Facts

- Milestone 1 is a requirements milestone.
- The repository has no accepted architecture, technology selection, or implementation specification yet.

## Requirements satisfied

- Product vision is documented.
- Requirements have stable identifiers.
- Requirements distinguish current foundation needs, artifact needs, Green Computing needs, traceability needs, and future automation needs.
- Initial traceability links requirements to current and future artifacts.

## Assumptions

- The product requirements baseline should remain technology-neutral.
- The first implementation scope will be selected after architecture and specifications exist.

## Risks

- Requirements may need refinement when real users provide feedback.
- Future automation could become too broad unless each implementation milestone remains small.

## Tradeoffs

- The baseline defines broad ecosystem requirements without implementing tooling. This slows visible functionality but preserves the required engineering order.

## Recommendation

Proceed to Milestone 2: architecture baseline for the requirements accepted in Milestone 1.
